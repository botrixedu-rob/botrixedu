Robotics Roadmap

6-Month Complete Robotics Curriculum

Every month is a new milestone — from zero electronics knowledge to building your own AI-powered robot and presenting it.

Month 1
Electronics Fundamentals
  • Voltage, Current, Resistance basics
  • Components: Resistor, LED, Capacitor
  • Circuit diagrams & Breadboard
  • Series & Parallel connections
  • LED Blinking & Push Button control
✓ Build basic electrical circuits
Month 2
Introduction to Robotics
  • Types of Robots & Robot Anatomy
  • Sensors & Actuators
  • Real-world Robotics applications
  • Simple Robot Assembly
  • Real Robot Analysis
✓ Understand how robots work
Month 3
Scratch Coding — Logic Building
  • Programming basics: logic & flow
  • Loops, Conditions, Variables
  • Event-driven programming
  • Animation creation & Game design
  • Mini Game Development
✓ Strong coding logic & creativity
Month 4
3D Design & Mechanical Thinking
  • Tinkercad & Fusion 360 basics
  • Gears, Levers, Joints mechanics
  • Design a robot chassis
  • 3D modelling for robotics parts
✓ Design thinking + mechanical skills
Month 5
Arduino & Sensors — Core Robotics
  • Arduino Uno introduction
  • Digital & Analog programming
  • Sensors: IR, Ultrasonic, LDR, LCD
  • Motors & Servo control
  • Mini Arduino projects
✓ Real working smart robot systems
Month 6
Python, C++ & AI — Final Project
  • Python fundamentals & C++ basics
  • AI Basics: Object Detection, Image Recognition
  • ChatGPT / Gemini AI tool automation
  • Final robotics project development
  • Project presentation & certification
✓ Build & present your own AI robot
